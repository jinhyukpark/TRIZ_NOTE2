TRIZ NOTE / PRIVACY

Privacy policy

What we process and why.

Effective · September 26, 2026

1. Operator and contact

TRIZ Note is operated by TeraGraph. Contact manager@teragraph.io to request access, correction, deletion or restriction, or ask privacy questions. We may verify identity to the extent needed to process a request.

2. Account and authentication

Email registration processes your email and authentication credentials through Supabase Auth. Google sign-in may provide your Google account identifier, email and basic profile such as name and profile image for authentication. The app does not receive your Google password. Login sessions are managed in device secure storage.

3. Learning and subscription data

We store and synchronize bookmarks, completion records, notes and account identifiers. Preferences such as language and some learning materials may remain on the device. Subscription verification processes store, product and transaction identifiers, expiry and entitlement status. Apple or Google processes payment methods; our app server does not directly store card numbers.

4. Purposes

Data supports authentication, learning synchronization, entitlement checks, content delivery, troubleshooting, abuse prevention and support. Support correspondence includes the email and information you send. Do not send passwords, card numbers, identity documents or unnecessary sensitive information.

5. Providers and international processing

Supabase provides authentication, databases and content storage in its Seoul, South Korea region. Google provides Google sign-in and hosts this public website. Apple and Google manage their respective store payments and subscriptions. When you use Google sign-in, your selected account identifier, email and basic profile are transmitted over encrypted connections at sign-in. Google and Apple may process information on global infrastructure under their privacy policies and contractual safeguards. Core app account and learning data are stored in the Supabase Seoul region.

6. Retention and deletion

Account data, learning records, notes, bookmarks and entitlement associations are retained while the account is active and removed from active server data when deletion completes. Security and troubleshooting logs are kept for up to 90 days, support correspondence for up to three years after resolution, and deleted data in rotating backups ordinarily for no more than 30 days. Records required by law may be isolated for the required period. Store transaction records follow Apple or Google policies and legal obligations.

7. Your rights and account deletion

Use Settings → Delete account or contact support. Google-only accounts or users unable to complete in-app identity checks can use email support. Account and cloud note, progress and bookmark deletion cannot be reversed. Device data and caches may require separate clearing, logout or app removal. Cancel subscriptions separately in the relevant store.

8. Website and safeguards

This website has no additional sign-in, advertising trackers or analytics tools. Hosting providers may process request data for delivery and security. The app uses HTTPS, device secure storage and account-based data access controls. No safeguards guarantee absolute security.

9. Children and updates

TRIZ Note is not designed for children under 14. If we learn that we hold personal data from a child under 14, we will verify and delete it. We update this page and any required app notices when processing changes, and provide advance notice of material changes. Effective date: September 26, 2026.
