TRIZ NOTE / DELETION

Delete your account

Deleting an account and cancelling a subscription are separate actions.

Effective · September 26, 2026

Request in the app

Open Settings → Delete account and complete verification. If you cannot complete the in-app checks, including for Google-only accounts, use the email route below.

Request without the app

Email manager@teragraph.io from your account email with the subject “TRIZ Note account deletion request”. Do not send passwords or identity documents. Support will explain the minimum verification and next steps.

Data covered

Deletion covers your account and associated cloud notes, completion records, bookmarks and entitlement associations. Deleted learning records cannot be recovered. Device caches and local materials may need separate removal.

Separate records

App Store and Google Play retain records under their own policies. Account deletion does not stop subscription renewals: cancel in the store first. Security and troubleshooting logs may be retained for up to 90 days, rotating backups ordinarily for up to 30 days, and support correspondence for up to three years after resolution. Legally required records may be isolated for the required period.
