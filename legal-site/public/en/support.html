Support

Get help with TRIZ Note and your account.

Effective · September 26, 2026

Contact us

manager@teragraph.io · TeraGraph TRIZ Note support. Include your app version, iOS or Android version, affected screen and steps to reproduce the problem. Remove personal and payment information from screenshots.

Manage subscriptions

Use subscription management in app settings or your App Store/Google Play account. Request refunds through the store where you paid. Never email card numbers or passwords.

Deletion and privacy requests

You can email support even without access to the app. Send “TRIZ Note account deletion request” from the email associated with your account. We verify account ownership before processing and never ask for your Google password. See the deletion page for scope.
